Hey there, I'm Talia

I became a product designer because I’ve always been curious about why some experiences feel more confusing or frustrating than they need to be. Design gives me a way to look beneath the surface, understand the people and systems behind a problem, and turn complexity into experiences that feel clear, thoughtful, and useful.

That same curiosity now shapes how I explore AI. I use it to quickly understand unfamiliar domains, bring ideas to life through interactive demos, and automate repetitive design tasks, so I can spend more time solving the problems that truly matter.
